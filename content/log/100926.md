Shobhan talked about how the ritual would change if certain aspects were different:

How would the experience be different if the journaling happened in public in front of people, rather than in private?
- perhaps it would feel more monutental, because it is a shared community experience

Instead of the writer wanting to remember the details of their month, what would change about the ritual if they wanted to forget these details? 
- perhaps writing, then putting memorabilia/stickers over the words to cover them, then putting it away, then looking at the calendar to seal the moment would be the steps instead (re-ordering the ritual's steps) 

How could the significance of this ritual be heightened? 
- the book being the first thing i bought when i came to college, and me telling myself that I will record my college experience in this journal, and record the details of my experience every month
- the pen being from my boyfriend, he gave his best pen to me when I didn't have one
- maybe I could wrap the journal in a special cloth, and unraveling it in a specific way. Or placing/taking it out of a significant box/drawer/space
