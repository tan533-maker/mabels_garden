Last night, #Armita got locked out of her room for the second time after being locked out the day before, and had to get the security to come and unlock the door. She left her keys in her room and locked the door after **she** left.

While she was explaining to me how she locked herself out again, I began to wonder how this #problem could be utilized in the OUR Scholar research project. One #idea that I thought of was this: When you come back to your apartment and open your door, an 'arm' drops down in front of you with a key holder of some sort. This arm holds at the junction of the door frame and the actual door, acting as an elbow that can open and close, moving the key holder up and down. When you close the door, this 'arm' closes back up, and keeps your keys on the wall next to the door.!
