Notes I took while listening 'The Digital Human Series 12: RItuals', a podcast Shobhan referred us to listen to.

- Habit- repeated behavior
- Ritual- actions/words that have meaning
- Things use ‘rituals’ to make things feel magical, it’s a habit
	- (Ex. Shower is a habit not ritual. If shower is the thought of rising you clean of negativity, space of release/reset, then it’s a ritual)
* Underlying psychology
	* Ex. Couples- not in a relationship until you announce it on social media. Important forum for manifestation. Take away symbol of being single, by changing it you go through a transformation. 
	* Making it public, the emotions, it is a ritual. Clear before and after
* Moments in life, clear before vs after, are the moments where rituals can take place
